## About me

I'm an industrial engineer who writes code. My Computer Programming background gives me the tools to build things, and my industrial engineering training gives me the habit of asking whether a process should work this way at all. I like problems where analysis, optimization and a clean user experience meet: supply chain, business development, operational efficiency and decision-support systems.

Right now I'm spending most of my free time on **large language models**: how they are trained and fine-tuned, and how to make them run efficiently.

## Featured project

**[WinLoud](https://github.com/Maslyon/WinLoud)** is a free, open-source system-wide sound booster and equalizer for Windows 10/11, built with C#, WPF and WASAPI. It has a 10-band EQ, bass boost and a distortion-safe limiter.

I keep a lot of other work private until I'm sure it is reliable enough for other people to depend on. I'd rather publish fewer things that hold up.

## Open source and volunteering

I think open source is one of the best ways to learn: you read other people's decisions, get your work questioned and leave something others can reuse. Volunteering works the same way for me. It is where skills become useful to someone else. I'm interested in contributing to projects where an engineering mindset helps, such as tooling, accessibility and process automation.
